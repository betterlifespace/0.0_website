Real Estate

Selling a home you've lived in for decades is more than a transaction. It's a life chapter. We treat it that way.

What It Is

BetterLifeSpace provides licensed real estate services specifically focused on Maturing Americans navigating a life transition. This isn't generic home-selling — it's real estate with the full context of what you're going through, handled by someone who understands both the market and the emotions.

Who It's For

  • Maturing Americans selling a long-time family home
  • Adult children helping aging parents navigate a home sale
  • Veterans and military families in the North Mississippi / Memphis area
  • Families transitioning to LifeCare or senior living facilities

What We Do

  • Comprehensive market analysis for your home and area
  • Strategic pricing that respects both market value and your timeline
  • Coordination with staging, transition, and disposition services (all under one roof)
  • Deep relationships with LifeCare facilities throughout the region
  • Handle showings, negotiations, and closing with care and transparency
  • Integration with the BetterLifeSpace client portal for full visibility

Why BetterLifeSpace

Steve Whitney isn't just a licensed agent — he's a retired Air Force Lieutenant Colonel who spent nearly 30 years managing housing, relocations, and the human care of entire military installations. His first real estate transaction was helping his own aging parents sell their two-story home. He understands what's at stake because he's lived it.

When you work with BetterLifeSpace, you get a single point of contact for everything — real estate, moving, staging, and disposition. No juggling vendors. No falling through the cracks.
