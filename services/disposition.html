Property Disposition (Re-Home)

A lifetime of belongings deserves more than a dumpster. We find the right home for things that matter.

What It Is

Property disposition — what we call "Re-Homing" — is the thoughtful process of finding the right next home for belongings that can't make the move with you. This isn't about getting rid of things. It's about honoring a lifetime of memories while helping families move forward.

Who It's For

  • Maturing Americans transitioning to a smaller space
  • Families managing an estate after a loved one's passing
  • Adult children coordinating a parent's move and belongings
  • Anyone facing rooms full of a lifetime and not knowing where to start

What We Do

Estate Sales

  • On-site estate sales — coordinated, priced, and managed with care
  • Professional presentation that respects every item's history
  • Revenue recovery for the family
  • Full cleanup after the sale

Marketplace (Facebook, etc.)

  • Listing items on Facebook Marketplace and similar local platforms
  • Photography, descriptions, and fair pricing
  • Managing inquiries, negotiations, and sales
  • Safe handoff logistics so you don't have to deal with strangers

eBay & Online Venues

  • Specialty and higher-value items listed on eBay and other online venues
  • Professional photos and detailed descriptions
  • Shipping coordination — we handle the logistics
  • Broader reach for unique items that deserve the right buyer

Why BetterLifeSpace

Every item tells a story. The dining table where holidays happened. The lamp that sat in the same corner for 40 years. The collection that took a lifetime to build. We understand the emotional weight of these decisions, and we handle them with the dignity they deserve.

No judgment. No rush. We've heard it all — and we're still here.
